What is RERA? Your Rights, Registrations, and How to Verify Projects?

Category: Blog   •   November 29, 2025

In the past, many homebuyers in India were unsure about their experience in the real estate market. Delays in getting possession, vague details about projects, and unexpected changes in pricing made home buying anxious. The launch of RERA by the government changed that experience and made the real estate industry more transparent and accountable. When buyers clearly understand what is RERA in real estate, they can make informed choices and verify every detail before investing.

Ruparel Realty has seen how clarity builds confidence. When customers understand what is RERA in property, they no longer rely only on verbal commitments. They now turn to RERA for verified information, approved plans, and timelines for the project. This blog post will provide information about what is RERA registration, what is the full form of RERA, and how every buyer can check project authenticity in just a few steps.

What Is RERA and Why Was It Introduced?

The main question that every buyer will have is "What is RERA?" RERA is the Real Estate Regulation and Development Act. It was enacted in 2016 to create regulations for the entire housing market in India. RERA encompasses the controls on what is allowed to happen in the housing market. When someone inquires about the full form of RERA, the answer provides a detailed definition, creating regulations around real estate activities and ensuring that homebuyers are protected.


Before RERA, many purchasers had difficulty determining the status of their investments and where their funds had been directed. By understanding what RERA is with respect to property, purchasers now realise that developers are subject to regulations that require them to be truthful about what is happening with their projects, and to consistently provide updates on the progress of any given development.

What Is RERA Registration and Why Does It Matter?

As soon as individuals comprehend what is RERA, they will often want to know what is RERA registration. All residential and commercial developments above a minimum threshold size are required to register under RERA before promoting, marketing, or securing a deposit for the sale of the property. Being aware of what RERA registration provides purchasers with peace of mind due to the assurance of:

  • Approved project layout and plans

  • Clear possession timelines

  • Transparent financial usage

  • Regular updates on the official RERA portal

When someone asks what is RERA in property, the answer is not only about paperwork. It is about ensuring the project has been legally verified and is monitored throughout construction. Ruparel Realty follows strict compliance so that every homebuyer can trust the information provided.

Gaining an understanding of what is RERA in real estate also means that if a project has not been registered with RERA, the project cannot be promoted or marketed. Therefore, purchasers will not be able to enter into a contract with respect to a development that is not approved, and may also face possible future legal difficulties.

What Are Your Rights Under RERA?

A major benefit of learning what is RERA is understanding buyer rights. Although many individuals perceive RERA as being nothing more than a registration requirement. A deeper understanding of what is RERA provides new homebuyers with the following additional rights under the Law: 

  • Receive accurate information on carpet area and pricing

  • Access approved plans and any changes made

  • Receive timely possession as stated in the agreement

  • Claim compensation for delays

  • Get advance payments limited to 10% before the agreement

This is why understanding what is RERA in property is essential. It gives buyers control and protects them from unexpected changes. When customers clearly understand what is the full form of RERA, they also understand the larger goal - regulation and development that benefits everyone.

Ruparel Realty guides buyers with complete transparency so they feel secure at every step, from booking to handover.

How to Verify RERA-Registered Projects?

After someone has an understanding of what a RERA registration is, they need to learn how they can verify this information. Each state has created a unique RERA website for individuals to verify their project information and ensure that projects are registered legally and compliant with the rules.

Here is a simple way to verify:


  • Search for your state’s RERA website

  • Open the “Registered Projects” section

  • Type the project’s name or registration number

  • View official documents, approvals, and progress updates


An additional benefit of knowing what RERA is in relation to Real Estate is that the buyer knows that the websites also display any complaints or issues about the project and any updates or completion certifications regarding the project. Knowing what is RERA in Real Estate allows the buyer to no longer rely on just the written documents (brochures), but also on the information that is published on the RERA website.

Every customer who understands RERA can independently check whether a project follows the rules, and this creates trust in both the developer and the purchase decision.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is RERA, and why was it introduced?

RERA, or the Real Estate Regulation and Development Act, was established to promote transparency in real estate transactions, control the conduct of real estate developers, and offer safeguards to home purchaser’s rights throughout India.

2. What is RERA registration?

Before marketing or selling any real estate projects, prospective developers must get RERA registration from the appropriate regulatory authority. The registration process ensures that each project has all required documentation and timelines confirmed.

3. How can I verify if a project is RERA-registered?

To verify whether or not you have picked a project that is RERA registered, simply visit your local RERA website. Type the project name or registration number into their search function, and you will have access to that project's registration approval and any updates regarding its current legal standing.

4. What rights do homebuyers get under RERA?

Home purchasers who purchase through RERA are entitled to have possession of the property as soon as possible, receive a carpet area report for the project, receive a copy of the approved layout plans, know how much they will be charged for the purchase of the property, and receive compensation for any delays in property delivery.

5. What is the full form of RERA?

RERA refers to the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act and is a law that is designed to make sure that real estate transactions are handled fairly to protect both buyers and sellers of properties and homeowners, and developers.
